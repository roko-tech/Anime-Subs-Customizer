The first public release of **Anime Subs Customizer**: it restyles the dialogue in your anime `.ass` subtitles with your font and colour, leaves signs and songs alone, and keeps every original.

## New in 1.2.0

- **A normal Windows installer** in English and Arabic: per-user, with optional start-at-sign-in, upgrades while the app is running, and FFmpeg included for subtitle extraction. A portable ZIP is also available.
- **A first-run welcome** and a **confirmation before the first restyle of a folder**, showing how many files will change.
- **The app starts in your language** (the installer's choice, or your Windows display language), and every message, button and error is in English or Arabic, right-to-left in Arabic.
- **Safer originals:** a subtitle replaced by a newer release (Bazarr, Sonarr) becomes the saved original, so **Restore** brings back the newer release. Styled, empty or half-written content is never saved as an original. Restored files stay original until a new version arrives.
- **No tug-of-war** between two copies of the app with different settings on one library.
- **Plain-language problems:** the Issues panel explains Windows' Ransomware protection, files in use, unplugged drives and more.
- Help, *Report a problem*, *About*, an optional daily update check (off by default), and a crash window with *Copy details* instead of silent failures.

Full list: [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/roko-tech/Anime-Subs-Customizer/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md).

## Installing

Windows may warn that the installer "isn't commonly downloaded" or that it "protected your PC", because the app isn't code-signed yet. **[What to click, and how to check your download](https://github.com/roko-tech/Anime-Subs-Customizer/blob/master/docs/install-help.md).** On Windows 11 PCs with Smart App Control turned on, the unsigned installer may be blocked; the guide explains your options.

## Known issues

- **Size presets can look different between fonts.** Players size text by a font's line spacing, so a font with unusually tall or short spacing comes out bigger or smaller at the same S / M / L. Adjust it with **− / +**; the app remembers it per font.
- **Older subtitle files without a `ScaledBorderAndShadow` setting:** the app adds it so its own outline scales with the video, and some players then draw the signs' outlines a little thicker too. **Restore originals…** brings the file back exactly.
- **The preview is close to, not identical to, your player:** outlines can look thinner, and a full stop at the end of an Arabic line can sit at the other end in players.
- Windows' own windows (the folder and colour pickers, the notification-area menu) follow Windows' language and direction.

All open issues: [github.com/roko-tech/Anime-Subs-Customizer/issues](https://github.com/roko-tech/Anime-Subs-Customizer/issues).

## Verify your download

- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the SHA-256 of every file.
- Each file has a GitHub build-provenance attestation: `gh attestation verify AnimeSubsCustomizer-Setup.exe -R roko-tech/Anime-Subs-Customizer`
- `sbom.cdx.json` lists the Python packages of the build (a CycloneDX SBOM); `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt` lists everything bundled, including FFmpeg, Python and Tcl/Tk, with their licences.
- FFmpeg is built from source for this app, under the LGPL, and its exact source is attached to the release. See `FFMPEG-SOURCE.md`.

## مشاكل معروفة

- **قد تختلف أحجام S / M / L بين الخطوط.** تحسب المشغّلات حجم النص من تباعد أسطر الخط، فيظهر الخط الذي تباعد أسطره كبير أو صغير على غير المعتاد أكبر أو أصغر. اضبطه بالزرّين **− / +**، ويتذكّر التطبيق الضبط لكل خط.
- **ملفات الترجمة القديمة التي تخلو من إعداد ‎`ScaledBorderAndShadow`‎:** يضيفه التطبيق ليتناسب حدّه مع حجم الفيديو، فترسم بعض المشغّلات حدود اللافتات أعرض قليلًا أيضًا. ويعيد **استعادة النسخ الأصلية…** الملف كما كان تمامًا.
- **المعاينة قريبة من المشغّل لا مطابقة له:** قد تبدو الحدود أرفع، وقد تظهر النقطة في آخر السطر العربي في الطرف الآخر داخل المشغّلات.
- نوافذ ويندوز نفسها (منتقي المجلد ومنتقي اللون وقائمة منطقة الإعلام) تتبع لغة ويندوز واتجاهه.

كل المشاكل المفتوحة: [github.com/roko-tech/Anime-Subs-Customizer/issues](https://github.com/roko-tech/Anime-Subs-Customizer/issues).
